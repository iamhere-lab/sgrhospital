FAQs

What services do you offer?

We provide a comprehensive range of skin, hair, and dental treatments, including but not limited to:

  • Skin care treatments like acne treatment, chemical peels, and laser hair removal.
  • Hair care treatments including PRP therapy, hair transplants, and scalp treatments.
  • Dental services such as routine check-ups, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, and dental implants.
How can I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling our clinic directly or through our website’s online booking system.

What should I expect during my first visit?

During your first visit, you will have a consultation with one of our specialists to discuss your concerns and goals. We will then develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Are the treatments safe?

Yes, all our treatments are performed by highly trained professionals using state-of-the-art equipment and FDA-approved products to ensure your safety and comfort.

How long do the treatments take?

The duration of treatments varies depending on the specific service. Routine dental check-ups can take around 30 minutes, while more extensive procedures like hair transplants or skin treatments may take longer.

Do you provide follow-up care?

Yes, we provide follow-up care to ensure the effectiveness of the treatments and your satisfaction. Our team is always available to address any concerns post-treatment.
